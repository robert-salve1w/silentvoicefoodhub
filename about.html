Silent Voice Food Hub

Our Story

Silent Voice Food Hub was created to address the challenges faced by students and staff at ISATU, such as crowded canteens, limited food choices, and the difficulty of finding meals that meet individual dietary needs.

What We Do

We provide convenient access to affordable Filipino meals through a QR-based ordering platform that allows customers to explore menus, view food information, and place orders with ease.

Our Mission

More than a food service, we are also an inclusive social enterprise dedicated to creating employment opportunities for deaf adults. Through food, technology, and community, we aim to promote convenience, nourishment, and equal opportunities for everyone.
